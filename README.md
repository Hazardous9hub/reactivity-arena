# ⚗️ Alchemix 3D &mdash; Metals & Non-Metals Interactive Learning Arcade
> **CBSE Class 10 Science (Chapter 3) &bull; 3D WebGL Experience &bull; Gamified Board Exam Prep**

An interactive, 3D scrollable learning platform designed for Class 10 students. Accessible instantly by scanning a QR code, featuring LinkedIn-style educational puzzles, Three.js WebGL visualizations, and CBSE board examination revision tools.

---

## 🌟 Features at a Glance

1. **LinkedIn-Style & Arcade Games**:
   - 🎯 **Pinpoint**: 5-step clue deduction challenges guessing the secret element or compound.
   - 🧗 **Crossclimb**: Dynamic Reactivity Series ladder climb.
   - 🧩 **Chemical Crossword**: Class 10 Board Exam edition with auto-validation.
   - 🔤 **Guess Word (Chemle)**: Wordle-style chemistry term guessing with board fact reveals.
   - 🧪 **Reaction Sorter**: SciChamp & Wordwall inspired drag-and-drop category sorter (Water reactions, Roasting vs Calcination).

2. **3D WebGL & Interactive Visualizations**:
   - 🔬 **Ionic Crystal & Electron Transfer** (NaCl & MgCl2 3D lattice).
   - ⚡ **Electrolytic Copper Refining Cell** (Anode dissolution, pure cathode deposit, anode mud).
   - 💥 **Thermit Welding Simulation** (Highly exothermic reaction for railway joints).

3. **Smooth Scrolling & Motion**:
   - **Lenis Smooth Scroll** for 60fps inertial scrolling.
   - **GSAP & ScrollTrigger** cinematic animations and camera movements.
   - Animated glowing cyberpunk neon chemistry logo.

4. **Frictionless QR Code Sharing**:
   - Dynamic client-side QR Code Generator modal with one-click print and image download for classroom and homework sheets.

5. **CBSE Board Exam High-Yield Traps**:
   - Past 7-year board questions, marking scheme breakdowns, and critical exception alerts (e.g. $\text{HNO}_3$ oxidation, amphoteric reactions).
